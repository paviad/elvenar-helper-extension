Move Plan NEW IN 11.0

Arrange your city in the planner, then choose City ▸ Send Move Plan to Game. The helper's Plan tab (Alt+C, L) lists the moves that turn your city into that layout, in an order the game will accept.

  • Drags that swap buildings in one go, street tiles to sell and streets to lay, upgrades, and buildings to place from the inventory.
  • Each line ticks itself off as you make the move. Do something the plan did not ask for and the steps left are worked out again.
  • Every step has a map drawn with the game's own pictures: the buildings to move picked out, and a see-through copy where each one goes.

ElvenAssist never makes a move itself. Every move is yours to make.

City Planner

Plan Your City

Your city, straight from the game. Rearrange it freely before you touch a single building.

Live City Planner

No import, no typing. Drag buildings around, drop one on another to swap them, change levels with +/- or remove one with Delete, unlock expansions, and undo any of it with Ctrl+Z.

Top-down, isometric and table views, so you can see the plan the way the game draws it.

Culture Bonus at a Glance

A bar under the culture row is notched at each culture bonus level and filled to where your layout stands. Hover it for the bonus you get and the culture the next level needs.

Upgrade Finder

Finds the event buildings sitting in your inventory that beat what you already have placed — only the swaps that give up nothing, ranked by what they gain per square, including Tome buildings.

Evolving buildings are measured at the stage your artifacts can actually reach.

Build Menu

Add buildings to your plan from a categorized catalog. Plan for future chapters before you even unlock the buildings.

In-game helper

Help While You Play

A panel inside the game that reads what you open and keeps up as you play.

KP Swaps

Never lose track of a knowledge point swap again. Your debts are tallied from the threads you post in and grouped by the player you owe, with a bar for how much of each you have repaid.

It shows how much room each of your wonders has to be asked for, and the KP instants you hold.

Tournament Counters

Open a province on the tournament map and see the squad to field against it — a blend across all five slots, worked out from the game's own unit data, sized to your squad size and checked against the troops you hold.

It also names the tournament coming up and what to train for it.

Cauldron Optimizer

Open the Witch's Cauldron and the Cauldron Optimizer is filled in for you: your diplomas, this week's premium ingredients, the effects you switched on, your wonder level and your Witch Points. Each city gets a world of its own there.

Tracking

Know What You Have

Your inventory, trades and Fellowship Adventure, laid out where you can search them.

Smart Inventory

Search, sort and filter your inventory instantly, and find that lost event building in seconds. Everything inside your Tomes is listed too, at what it gives in your chapter.

Copy Table sends the list to a spreadsheet.

Fellowship Adventure

Track badge progress in real time, and see a production timeline that tells you exactly when your badges will be ready.

Efficient Trades

Spots the lucrative offers for you: non-boosted ascended goods traded for your boosted ones.

And More

Screenshot & Export

Save your whole city as a PNG with labels, ready to paste into Discord, or export it for the Chapter 25 planner.

Chat & Messages

Guild chat with search and jump to first unread, and your Inbox and Outbox with search across threads. Alt+C opens it.

Always Up to Date

Updated for the newest chapter as soon as it lands, with the building sizes and assets others are missing.

Safe & Open Source
